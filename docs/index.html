هل يحتاج التطبيق إلى Python أو VLC؟

لا. الملف التنفيذي والمثبّت يحتويان على محرّك VLC كامل وكل الاعتماديات. يكفي Windows 10/11 (64-bit).

هل التطبيق مجاني؟

نعم، التطبيق مجاني للاستخدام الشخصي. الكود منشور للاطّلاع فقط بموجب ترخيص احتكاري (All Rights Reserved)، راجع صفحة الترخيص في المستودع.

هل يعمل مع قارئ الشاشة الذي أستخدمه؟

نعم، تم اختباره مع NVDA وNarrator، ويتبع معايير إتاحة Qt التي تدعم JAWS أيضًا. كل العناصر تعلن أسماءها واختصاراتها.

Accessible by design

Accessibility is not an add-on; it is the foundation of every screen and every control.

  • Full NVDA, JAWS and Narrator support.
  • Every control announces its name, shortcut and description.
  • The seek slider is its own Tab stop and announces elapsed, total and remaining time in words.
  • High-contrast themes and scalable fonts.
  • Complete keyboard navigation without a mouse.
  • No drag-and-drop dependency, avoiding screen-reader conflicts.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you download.

Do I need Python or VLC?

No. The executable and installer bundle the complete VLC engine and every dependency. Windows 10/11 (64-bit) is all you need.

Is it free?

Yes, the application is free for personal use. The source is published for viewing only under a proprietary (All Rights Reserved) licence; see the licence page in the repository.

Does it work with my screen reader?

Yes. It has been tested with NVDA and Narrator and follows Qt accessibility standards, which also support JAWS. Every control announces its name and shortcut.
